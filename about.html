About Harness Logic

Not a hobby wiring shop

Harness Logic is a Melbourne-based precision electrical engineering and custom wiring harness fabrication business. We exist for the projects that need serious engineering discipline applied at small and custom volumes — EV conversions, prototype electronics, and specialist vehicle systems that larger manufacturers won't take on.

Philosophy

Engineering rigour, applied where volume manufacturers won't go

Small and custom jobs are often treated as an afterthought by manufacturers built for scale. We take the opposite approach: the same design discipline, testing standards, and documentation you'd expect from a high-volume production line, applied to one-off builds, prototypes, and low-volume runs. That's underpinned by real project history across mining equipment, heavy electric vehicles, and specialist control systems — not just enthusiasm for wiring.
