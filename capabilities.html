Capabilities

A workshop built for demanding electrical work

PCB Reflow Oven & Rework

Controlled reflow soldering and rework for electronics assembly.

PCB Inspection Microscope

Fine-detail inspection for board-level quality control.

Test & Measurement Equipment

HiPot Tester

High-potential testing for EV/HV harness verification — a capability that sets us apart for high-voltage work.
