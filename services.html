Services

From schematic to fabricated harness

Four core services, all delivered with the same engineering discipline whether the job is a one-off prototype or a low-volume production run.

01

Custom Wiring Harness Fabrication

Prototype through low-volume production, designed and built in-house with the same rigour applied at every quantity.

  • Harness design for automotive, EV, and industrial applications
  • Prototype through low-volume production runs
  • Automotive HV and LV harness fabrication
  • Connector selection, crimping, and termination to spec
  • In-house testing before delivery
02

Electrical System Design & Schematics

Circuit design and documentation that gives your project a clear, manufacturable electrical foundation.

  • Circuit design and schematic capture
  • Wiring diagrams and manufacturability documentation
  • Component selection and sourcing guidance
  • Design review for existing electrical systems
03

Prototyping & Low-Volume Runs

Fast iteration for startups and product developers who need a partner comfortable with early-stage uncertainty.

  • Rapid prototyping of harnesses and electrical assemblies
  • Design-for-manufacture feedback during development
  • Low-volume production without the volume-manufacturer minimums
  • Iteration support as designs evolve
04

Engineering Consulting & Support

Independent engineering input where you need it — from design review through to post-fabrication support.

  • Design review and troubleshooting
  • Electrical safety guidance
  • Testing and validation support, including HiPot and insulation resistance testing
  • Post-fabrication support
